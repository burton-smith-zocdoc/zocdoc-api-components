# Accordion

A vertically stacked set of headings that reveal or hide their sections of content. Use it for
content that most people don't need at once — FAQs, insurance details, visit policies.

## Default

Put `zd-accordion-item` elements in the default slot. Each item manages its own open state.

<code-bubble>

```html
<zd-accordion>
  <zd-accordion-item heading="What should I bring to my visit?">
    Bring your photo ID and your insurance card.
  </zd-accordion-item>
  <zd-accordion-item heading="How do I reschedule?">
    Open the appointment from your account and choose a new time.
  </zd-accordion-item>
</zd-accordion>
```

</code-bubble>

## Single expand mode

By default any number of items can be open at once. Add the `open-single` attribute to close the
other items whenever one opens.

<code-bubble>

```html
<zd-accordion open-single>
  <zd-accordion-item heading="Before your visit">Arrive 10 minutes early.</zd-accordion-item>
  <zd-accordion-item heading="After your visit">
    Your visit summary appears in your account.
  </zd-accordion-item>
</zd-accordion>
```

</code-bubble>
